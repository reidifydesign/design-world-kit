## Users

Neighbours and small local businesses who want a few dozen to a few hundred printed pieces, such as invitations, menus and shop cards, and who are happy to visit the shop or wait for pickup.

## Product Purpose

Lantern Post is a small letterpress print shop. It sets and prints short runs on paper stock the owner has tested. Success is a customer who comes back with the next job.

## Positioning

Every job is set and printed by the same person who takes the order, in the same building.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Letterpress and foil on cotton and uncoated stock.
- Short runs only. The minimum run is 50 cards.
- No full-colour photographic printing.
- Terms to use: "run", "stock", "proof". Not "batch", "paper type".

## Brand Commitments

The name is Lantern Post. The wordmark is set in lowercase. There is no mascot.
