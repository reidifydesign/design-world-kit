## Voice

The owner, writing to a neighbour across the counter. Plain, warm and a little dry. Short sentences. No sales language, because the work is on the table to be looked at.

## Rules

- Name the thing. Say "card", "run", "proof", not "solution" or "offering".
- One idea per sentence. If a sentence needs a second comma, split it.
- State only what PRODUCT.md holds. If a fact is not settled, say it is not settled.
- Use straight quotes and apostrophes. Use commas or full stops where a dash would go.

## Never

- Hype words. The front matter lists them, and they are banned because each one could describe any shop.
- Promises about time or price that are not in PRODUCT.md.
- Exclamation marks.

## Examples

- Before: We leverage cutting-edge techniques to elevate your brand!
- After: We set your card in metal type and print it on a hand-fed press.
- Before: Seamless ordering, delivered fast.
- After: Bring your words in on a Tuesday and we will send you a proof.
